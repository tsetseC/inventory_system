INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

All charts below are built only from StockMovement table using RFID scans.

Daily Units Sold (My Data)

Aggregated from OUT movements in StockMovement captured via RFID.

What this shows: This line shows how many items were sold each day using RFID scans.
Why it matters: It helps managers quickly see busy days, slow days, and overall sales trends.
Example: If the line spikes on weekends, it means more customers buy on weekends.

Units Sold by Item (My Data)

Total units sold per item, based on OUT movements from RFID scans.

💡 Tip: click a bar to filter the Daily chart and Forecast chart by that item. Click on empty space in the bar chart to reset.

What this shows: A comparison of total units sold per item.
Why it matters: Helps identify fast-moving products and slow-selling stock.
How to use it: Click any bar to see that item’s daily sales and demand forecast below.

Live Stock Level (RFID Calculated)

Received (IN) − Sold (OUT) = In Stock. Reorder Qty uses a 7-day demand + 25% safety buffer.

Item Received (IN) Sold (OUT) In Stock Reorder Qty Status
How to read this table:
• Green “In stock” = safe quantity available.
• Low stock = item will soon run out.
• Oversold = more items were sold than recorded as received (data mismatch or missing IN scans).

Reorder Quantity: Automatically calculated using the last 7 days of sales plus a safety buffer to avoid stock-outs.

Forecast vs Actual Demand (Last 30 Days)

Actual demand is based on OUT movements in StockMovement. Forecast is a rolling 5-day average calculated from your own RFID sales history. When you click an item bar above, this chart focuses on that specific item.

Actual (green line): Real items sold based on RFID scans.
Forecast (blue line): A prediction based on recent sales trends.
Why it matters: If actual sales rise above forecast, you may run out of stock. If forecast stays higher than actual, you may be over-ordering and tying up cash in stock.

Machine Learning Forecast vs Actual Demand (Last 30 Days)

This chart uses a trained machine learning model (based on StockMovement history) to predict daily demand and compares it to what actually happened.

Actual (green line): Real units sold from RFID scans for each day.
ML Forecast (purple line): What the machine learning model expected you to sell on those days.
Why it matters: The closer the two lines are, the better the model is at predicting demand. Large gaps highlight days where the model under- or over-estimated sales.
